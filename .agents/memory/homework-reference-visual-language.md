The reference has three distinct visual modes: a compact light dashboard, a dark full-screen camera capture surface, and a dense solved-results page. Utility content should stay close together; avoid large marketing-style cards and excess header copy.

**Why:** Visual fidelity depends more on the contrasting screen modes, content density, and restrained hierarchy than on adding more features.

**How to apply:** When extending the homework flow, preserve the compact Home rhythm, keep scanner controls on the dark capture surface, and make answer/result cards concise with clear step groupings.
